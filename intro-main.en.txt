Happy Farmer — a slow-paced, relaxing farm management game (v1.1.0)

Published by leleleling Studio · Lelelegame. Rated Ages 6+.

Start with one small plot. Plant your first seed, watch it grow, harvest it, and fill your storage with the fruits of your work. That simple loop is the whole point of Happy Farmer.

WHAT YOU WILL DO
- Collect 1,000 crops across 10 families, from radish and cabbage to legendary divine grain
- Expand your farm endlessly; every new plot costs 25% more than the last
- Run a 9-level processing workshop with 1,893 goods to discover
- Trade with 5 merchants, each with different purchase rules
- Unlock 100 shop items in batches as you level up

FEATURES
- Weather and seasons: sunshine, light rain and storms affect growth
- Random events: treasure chests, thieves, crop disease, early merchants
- Consignment market: set your own price; unsold goods return after 7 days
- Auctions every 14 game days, with NPC bidders
- 50 farm skins that change the whole palette
- 150 achievements, including hidden ones, plus an achievement hall
- Save export / import so you can move progress between devices
- Progress is stored locally in your browser; the core game works offline. The optional leaderboard unlocks at level 5 and is opt-in

WHO IS IT FOR
- Fans of slow farming games such as Stardew Valley or Story of Seasons
- Anyone who wants a calm five-minute break
- Collectors who enjoy watching numbers grow

No stamina, no forced quests, no daily check-ins, no in-app purchases. Just plant, harvest, and get a little better every day.
